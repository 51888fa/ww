About GameVault

GameVault is a free online gaming portal dedicated to providing fun, accessible, and safe browser-based games for players of all ages. Founded in 2026, we offer a curated collection of 50+ HTML5 games across multiple genres.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to create a gaming destination where anyone can play great games instantly, without barriers. No downloads, no installations, no sign-ups - just click and play. We believe gaming should be accessible to everyone, regardless of device or technical expertise.

What We Offer

  • 50+ Free Games: A diverse collection spanning action, puzzle, racing, sports, arcade, shooter, .io games, and strategy categories.
  • Instant Play: All games run directly in your browser using HTML5 technology. No plugins or downloads required.
  • Cross-Platform: Play on desktop, laptop, tablet, or mobile phone. Our website is fully responsive.
  • Safe Environment: We review all games before adding them. No malware, no intrusive pop-ups, no malicious code.
  • Regular Updates: We continuously add new games to keep the experience fresh and exciting.

How It Works

GameVault aggregates publicly available HTML5 games and embeds them in our platform. Each game is categorized and described to help you find exactly what you are looking for. Simply browse by category, search by name, or explore our featured games section.

When you click on a game, it opens in a modal window where you can play immediately. You can also switch to fullscreen mode for a more immersive experience.
